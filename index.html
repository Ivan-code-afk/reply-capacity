How is this calculated?

Each day, new messages join the queue. We subtract as many replies as fit in your daily time budget. Formula: next queue = max(0, current queue + new messages − replies sent). The workload stays the same for all 7 days, including weekends. This is a scenario calculation, not a prediction of actual future messages.
